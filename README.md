## ⚠️ Major Problems Identified

1. **High Revenue Concentration**
   - 63% of revenue from only 2 products (Rice & Chicken)
   - High vulnerability to supply shocks or demand changes

2. **Underperforming Region**
   - Lagos (economic hub) is the lowest contributor despite huge market potential.

3. **Sales Team Inequality**
   - Significant performance gap between top (David) and bottom (Ibrahim) reps.

4. **Strong Seasonality**
   - Very weak January performance and low Monday sales.

5. **Limited Product Portfolio**
   - Many low-performing products taking up inventory and shelf space.

---

## ✅ Recommendations & Solutions

### Product Strategy
- **Diversify** product mix to reduce dependency on Rice and Chicken.
- Bundle top products (Rice + Chicken + Eggs + Oil).
- Review and potentially discontinue or reposition weak products (Soft Drinks, Bread, Sugar).
- Introduce new high-potential items based on customer feedback.

### Regional Strategy
- **Prioritize Investment in Kano** (already performing well).
- **Launch Lagos Recovery Plan**: better marketing, promotions, logistics, and rep incentives.
- Transfer best practices from Kano to Lagos.

### Sales Team Development
- Introduce performance-based incentives and commissions.
- Mentoring program (David to mentor lower performers).
- Regular sales training and territory optimization.

### Marketing & Operations
- Run **Weekend Promotions** (especially Friday & Thursday).
- Aggressive **January Campaign** to boost the weakest month.
- Implement ABC inventory analysis (Rice = A category).
- Develop customer loyalty program to increase AOV.

### Dashboard Enhancements
- Add **Profit Margins** per product/region.
- Include MoM/YoY growth rates.
- Track inventory levels, stockouts, and repeat customers.

## Conclusion

Fahad Store has a **strong foundation** with healthy total revenue and clear top performers. However, the business is currently **too concentrated** in a few products and regions.

**With disciplined execution** of the above recommendations especially diversification, Lagos improvement, and sales team optimization — the store is well-positioned for sustainable and balanced growth.
